# Career History

Amelia taught special education in Chicago for about three and a half years, then spent three roles in ed-tech startups in customer success and implementation. In 2024 she applied to business school. She is now at Berkeley Haas, graduating in May 2027, and returning full time to Workday in finance and strategy.

## Education

- **Vanderbilt University**, graduated 2018: Human and Organizational Development, minor in English literature.
- The English minor was earned at **Oxford University** during a semester abroad in 2017.
- **Berkeley Haas MBA**, scheduled to graduate **May 2027**.

## Teaching (June 2018 – November 2021)

- **Teach For America**: K–5 special education teacher in Chicago.
- About two years in **Chicago Public Schools**, teaching a lot of social-emotional education in Englewood, a primarily Black, low-income neighborhood facing poverty, gun violence and addiction.
- About two years at **Acero Marquez**, a charter school in the Acero network in the Back of the Yards neighborhood, which is mostly first-generation Mexican immigrant families.
- Taught through COVID: remote learning, then the return to in-person.

## Ed-tech startups

- **ClassClick**, Implementation Specialist (Nov 2021 – about Nov 2022): a company of about 30 people. She learned customer success, implementation, startups and product, and how teams fit together to build a product for schools.
- **Paper Education**, virtual tutoring (Nov 2022 – Aug 2023): about 600 employees and once valued at about $1 billion. Her role was eliminated because of external factors: the post-COVID ed-tech downturn, as schools lost funding and became stricter about purchases. Paper laid off about 33% of the company that year.
- **Beable**, Customer Success Manager, Strategic Accounts (from Jan 2024): founded by Saki Dodelson, who previously sold an ed-tech company for about $600 million. The role didn't challenge her in the ways she hoped, and she wanted a role outside education.

## Business school

- In 2024 she applied only to California schools: Stanford, Berkeley Haas and UCLA Anderson.
- She was admitted to Haas and Anderson, each with a $60,000 scholarship.
- She almost chose Anderson (she was living in San Diego and loved Southern California, and got married in LA around then). She chose Haas after visiting, inspired by the people, the Bay Area and its closeness to Silicon Valley.

## Workday

- **Summer 2026:** MBA intern on Workday's business finance team, working with the Global Partner Organization and specifically the **Marketplace** team, a new venture within Workday.
- Did financial modeling and projections for Marketplace and for **monetizing AI agents** in the platform, plus other strategy projects.
- Returning to Workday full time in summer or fall 2027, after graduation, to build a career in corporate tech, finance and strategy.
