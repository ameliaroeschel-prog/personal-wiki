# Evaluation Suite

A fixed set of test cases, scored the same way every time, used to compare a model before and after training or across experiments.

## Related concepts

- [[Corpus]] — evaluation items must stay out of the training data.
- [[Loss]] — training loss alone does not prove the model performs well on the evaluation.

## Appears in

- [[Custom nanoGPT LLM]] — a 48-case next-word exam with coverage, scorable accuracy and all-case success.
- [[Ms Pac-Man DQN Agent]] — the same five seeds at 5% exploration, before and after training.
