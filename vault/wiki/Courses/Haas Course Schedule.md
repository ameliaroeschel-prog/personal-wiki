# Haas Course Schedule

Amelia is taking five courses at Berkeley Haas. They range from economics and accounting to hands-on AI building and interpersonal skills. Source: a dictated note, so wording in the original is conversational.

## Strategy, Structure, and Incentives

- **Instructor:** Greg LeBlanc
- **Meets:** Tuesdays and Thursdays, 11:00 a.m.–12:30 p.m., room N570
- **Covers:** economics, markets and firms, organizational design.

## Fundamentals of Agentic AI

- **Instructor:** Pepe Alonso
- **Meets:** Tuesdays, 4–6 p.m., room N570
- **Covers:** large language models, from their historical context to the present day and what you can do with them, with a lot of hands-on building.
- This personal wiki project is being built for this class.

## Conflict Lab

- **Instructor:** Francesca LeBaron
- **Meets:** Thursdays, 2–5 p.m., room N500
- **Covers:** hands-on practice of difficult conversations at work, with elements borrowed from couples therapy. Recent topics: somatics, giving a good apology, giving feedback, and intent versus impact.

## Corporate Financial Accounting

- **Instructor:** Tanya Paul
- **Meets:** Thursdays, 8–11 a.m., room N170
- **Covers:** reading a 10-K and evaluating financial reports from an investor's perspective, including revenue recognition and how reporting choices can make a firm look more or less favorable.

## Power and Politics

- **Instructor:** Cameron Anderson
- **Meets:** Mondays and Wednesdays, 2–4 p.m., room N170
- **Covers:** influence at work — using relationships and networks to build credibility, advocacy and influence, and relating behaviors such as trustworthiness, personability and openness.
